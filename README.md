Dow Jones is a financial news and information company, publisher of The Wall Street Journal, Barron's, MarketWatch, and Dow Jones Newswires, and operator of the Factiva news archive and the Risk & Compliance data business. Its Developer Platform (developer.dowjones.com) publishes REST APIs for entity screening and monitoring, third-party risk, due-diligence reports, risk profile search, and news content (Newswires real-time search, top stories, calendars, newsletters), secured by the Dow Jones Identity Service (OAuth 2.0 / OIDC) or Factiva user keys.

**Areas:** Finance · Market Data · News · Publishing · Risk and Compliance · Screening · Due Diligence · Media Monitoring

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Dow Jones: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
