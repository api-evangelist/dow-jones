# Dow Jones

> Dow Jones is a financial news and information company (The Wall Street Journal, Barron's, MarketWatch, Factiva, Dow Jones Newswires, Risk & Compliance). Its Developer Platform exposes REST APIs for news content, screening, and third-party risk, secured by the Dow Jones Identity Service (OAuth 2.0 / OIDC) or Factiva user keys.

## APIs

- [Developer Platform](https://developer.dowjones.com/): portal, docs, and API reference
- [Getting started](https://developer.dowjones.com/documents/site-docs-getting_started): credentials, sessions and authentication
- [Risk & Compliance Screening and Monitoring API](https://developer.dowjones.com/documents/site-docs-risk_and_compliance_apis-screening_and_monitoring_api-batch_screening_api): case-based batch screening (api.dowjones.com)
- [Advanced Screening and Monitoring API](https://developer.dowjones.com/documents/site-docs-risk_and_compliance_apis-advanced-screening-and-monitoring-api): alerting and assessment workflows
- [Risk Search / Profiles / Taxonomy / Profile Version History APIs](https://developer.dowjones.com/documents/site-docs-risk_and_compliance_apis-risk_and_compliance_2_0-risk_search_api): Risk & Compliance 2.0 suite
- [Due Diligence Reports API](https://developer.dowjones.com/documents/site-docs-risk_and_compliance_apis-due_diligence_reports_api-risk_reports_api): order and retrieve risk reports
- [RiskCenter Third Party Platform API](https://developer.dowjones.com/documents/site-docs-risk_and_compliance_apis-third_party_api): third-party onboarding, monitoring, webhooks (api-thirdparty.riskcenter.dowjones.com)
- [Factiva Analytics](https://developer.dowjones.com/documents/factiva_integration-factiva_analytics): Snapshots, Streams, Time Series, Explain
- [Factiva Retrieval API](https://developer.dowjones.com/documents/factiva_integration-factiva_retrieval_api): GenAI/RAG article retrieval
- [Dow Jones Newswires APIs](https://developer.dowjones.com/documents/site-docs-newswires_apis-dow_jones_newswires_real_time_api-real_time_search_api): Real-Time Search, Top Stories, Calendar Live, Content
- [Factiva Workflow Toolkit](https://developer.dowjones.com/documents/factiva_integration-factiva_workflow_toolkit): Content, Newsletters, Search APIs

## Specs

- 17 OpenAPI/Swagger definitions harvested verbatim in openapi/ (Screening & Monitoring, Private Lists, Advanced Screening, Risk Search/Profiles/Taxonomy/History, Due Diligence Reports, RiskCenter Third Party 0.1/0.2, Content, Newsletters, Newswires Real-Time/Content, Top Stories, Calendar Live, Company News Radar)
- OIDC discovery: https://accounts.dowjones.com/.well-known/openid-configuration
