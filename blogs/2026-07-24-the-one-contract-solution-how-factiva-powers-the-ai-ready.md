The federal information landscape has a vendor problem. Most government agencies managing intelligence data workflows are dealing with a fragmented portfolio, cobbling together dozens of separate subscriptions to cover news, regulatory content, geopolitical analysis, and security intelligence. Each vendor has its own data architecture, integration challenges, and compliance and governance processes.
