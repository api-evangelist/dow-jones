Key Takeaways: This article analyzes the data on fines, number of enforcement actions, and other information since 2020 to determine the enforcement priorities for the current U.S. administration. This analysis, albeit preliminary, points to some trends, in particular an increasing number of BIS actions and FCA tariff settlements, and a drop in FCPA prosecutions.
